# Final UX corporate Administrator acceptance checklist

Use a legitimate corporate Administrator session only on the governed
candidate, staging, or post-activation surface.  This checklist does not
authorize a credential bypass and automated browser evidence is not a
substitute for this review.

## Normal user experience

- Confirm natural Machine, Tool, and EOAT ordering in Library; verify the
  compact Last Updated state and global entity/Settings search.
- Open Home, Library, global search, Relationships, Documents, Photos and a
  photo detail.  Verify readable labels, real thumbnails, downloads, metadata
  actions, and no internal storage paths.
- Walk Bulk Status Update through preview, required reason, and the explicit
  audited commit confirmation without changing non-test records.

## Administration

- Confirm exactly one active destination at a time; Data is only a grouping,
  Diagnostics is absent, and Return to EOAT Atlas is the final normal item.
- Review Overview, Users & Access, Group Access Policies, Settings, System,
  and Integrity & Evidence at desktop width for clipping, dead space, and
  understandable healthy/policy-disabled states.
- In Group Access Policies, verify role choices, required reason, preview and
  confirmation, recovery-path protection, audit receipt, and non-Administrator
  denial using approved test identities only.
- In Settings, verify the persisted global Library page-size setting, required
  reason and audit receipt; confirm browser preferences, Administrator-global
  settings, and deployment-only configuration remain clearly separated.

Record the candidate URL, authenticated Administrator identity, timestamp,
screens reviewed, and any acceptance decision in the governed release record.
