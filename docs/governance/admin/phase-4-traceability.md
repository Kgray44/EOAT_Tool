## Broader-capability boundary

Phase 4 deliberately stops at the accepted test-only grant shape. Phase 6 is
the prerequisite for any broader runtime privilege, production-like recovery,
or browser-executable operational capability, and requires a separate
approved authority, scope, and acceptance run.

## Explicit deferrals

Corporate identity/reauthentication is Phase 5. Production deployment,
production database privileges, NGINX, retention policy, production backups,
real restore approval, factory reset, data purge, destructive repair, and
security-mapping reset require project-owner, IT, and Quality approval and are
not implemented here.
